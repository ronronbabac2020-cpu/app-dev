# My Favorite Series: *Game of Thrones*

![Game of Thrones Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/d/d8/Game_of_Thrones_title_card.jpg)

## Overview
*Game of Thrones* is an **epic fantasy drama** television series created by *David Benioff* and *D. B. Weiss*, based on *George R. R. Martin’s* book series *A Song of Ice and Fire*.  
It aired from **2011 to 2019** and became one of the most talked-about shows in TV history.

---

## ⚔️ Main Themes
1. Power and betrayal  
2. Family and honor  
3. War and politics  
4. Fate and prophecy  

---

### 🏰 Main Characters
- **Jon Snow (Kit Harington)** – the honorable warrior of the North  
- **Daenerys Targaryen (Emilia Clarke)** – the Mother of Dragons  
- **Tyrion Lannister (Peter Dinklage)** – the witty and intelligent advisor  
- **Arya Stark (Maisie Williams)** – the brave assassin seeking justice
